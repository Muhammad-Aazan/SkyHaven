### Video Editor Pro Tips:
1. **Watermark:** Video ke top-right ya top-left corner mein `ad-assets/04_brand_assets/skyhaven_icon_mark.png` ko 40% opacity par rakhein.
2. **Logo Placement:** Ending screen par `skyhaven_logo_master.png` (light background par) ya `skyhaven_logo_white.png` (dark background par) use karein.
3. **Motion Graphics:** Motion video clips (`01_cloud_computing_motion.webm` aur `02_atmospheric_ambient_1080p.mp4`) ko background layers par 30-50% opacity aur dark gradient overlay ke sath lagayein taake UI screenshots khil kar samne aaein.
